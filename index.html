About Hailey

Meet the Person Behind the Paws!

Hi, I'm Hailey! I believe pets deserve more than just someone to feed them or take them outside. They deserve attention, affection, exercise, and someone who genuinely enjoys spending time with them.

Whether we're exploring a new trail, enjoying a neighborhood walk, or relaxing at home, my goal is to make every pet feel safe, comfortable, and loved.

I understand how important your pets are to you, and I want you to feel confident knowing they're in caring hands.

You work directly with Hailey. No rotating staff and no strangers. You get one person who learns your pet's routine and builds a lasting relationship with you both.

Safety & Peace of Mind

Your Pet's Safety Is Always a Priority.

Safe walks & adventures

Routes and trail lengths are matched to your pet's age, fitness and abilities, with leashes and fresh water along the way.

Weather conditions

Heat, cold, snow, ice and storms are taken seriously. Outings are shortened or adjusted when conditions call for it.

Emergency veterinary care

Your vet's details and emergency contact are on file before the first visit, so Hailey can act quickly if something comes up.

Medication & feeding

Your instructions are followed exactly, from portion sizes to medication timing.

Supervised interactions

Pets meet other animals only when they're compatible and you've agreed, and always under supervision.

Secure home access

Keys and door codes are kept private and your home is always left locked and secure.

Communication

You'll hear from Hailey after every visit, and right away if anything needs your attention.

Individual exercise needs

Puppies, seniors and high-energy dogs each get the kind of activity that's right for them.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer a free meet-and-greet?

Yes! Before your first booking, Hailey will meet you and your pet so everyone can get comfortable. Choose "Free meet-and-greet" in the booking form.

Can you care for multiple pets?

Yes! Additional pet fees are listed under each service.

Can you care for puppies and senior dogs?

Care can be discussed based on each pet's individual needs and abilities. Walks and adventures are paced to suit them.

Will I receive updates about my pet?

Yes. You'll get photos, short videos, and notes on feeding, activities and how your pet is doing, with a personalized update after each service.

Can you administer medication?

Add your pet's medications and instructions to their profile when you book. Hailey will confirm which ones she can give before your first visit.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Walks and outdoor activities are adjusted for safety, with shorter routes, cooler times of day or indoor play when needed. Hailey will let you know about any change.

Do you offer recurring walks?

Yes. Tick "Repeat this every week" in the booking form to set up a regular weekly walk without a new request each time.

Can my dog stay at your home?

Yes. Overnight boarding at Hailey's home is $70/night (+$10 per additional pet) and includes walks, playtime, outdoor adventures and feeding on your pet's routine. Hailey always has 1–2 extra crates on hand if needed. A meet-and-greet first helps make sure your dog is comfortable.

Can your dogs join my dog's activities?

Hailey may bring her two dogs along when appropriate and when you agree.

What is your cancellation policy?

Hailey's cancellation and refund policy will be shared with your booking confirmation. Questions? Get in touch.
